## Stage 8
- Live-enrolment rule (one shared definition, pinned by a test across C#, EF and SQL): Active enrolment whose outcome is not LEFT or TRANSFERRED_OUT, and not PASSED_OUT once its year is no longer current. Used in live rosters, counts, lists, reminders and billing. Year views of non-current years show everyone (history). tb_students.status is NOT changed when a student leaves or passes out (previous decision from stage 4 kept).
- 8a Record leaving (api/StudentLeaving, School Admin and Principal): type Left or Transferred out, leaving date (not future, not before enrolment), reason; same dues check and override as promotion (reason min 10 chars); writes outcome and leaving fields on the enrolment, revokes refresh tokens, logs in tb_student_leaving_log; correction path (refused if a later enrolment exists, year closed, outcome came from promotion, or a certificate is issued). Student login refused when Inactive, Left or Transferred out (and passed-out once the year is no longer current), at login and refresh; the already-issued 15-minute access token lives until expiry. Guardians keep read-only access; push fee reminders skip leavers; billing stops counting a leaver from the leaving date. A leaver's unpaid dues stay in fee money totals.
- 8b Transfer certificate: School Admin and Principal; only for a student whose latest enrolment is LEFT, TRANSFERRED_OUT or PASSED_OUT; number <school code>/TC/<academic year>/<4 digits> from a never-decreasing per-school counter under an advisory lock; frozen JSON snapshot rendered for every copy; duplicates keep the number and are marked "DUPLICATE COPY No. N"; every print is audited; dues override stored but never printed (PDF says Cleared or Not cleared); void keeps the row and number. Optional school fields (affiliation number, UDISE code, board school code) and optional typed certificate boxes (nationality, religion, category, qualified for promotion, working days, concession, date of application, conduct wording) live only in the certificate snapshot, never on tb_students. Layout is generic, not yet matched to a real CBSE or ICSE form (owner to supply a sample).
- 8c Section change (api/SectionChanges): same class and year only; updates the single enrolment in place and writes a history row; refused if the year has a published report card for that section, the year is closed, the enrolment is not live, or the section is full; one change at a time per school; marks, report rows and class-test results follow the student; class teacher and rank are per section.
- 8d Web: /student-leaving, /section-change, "Section changes" card on the student profile, transfer certificates page; sidebar entries after Promotion.
