An item's quantity is never typed in or edited. It only changes when something happens: stock is received, items are issued or returned, or an adjustment is recorded. Every change is written to a permanent history, so the number on screen can always be explained, and it can never go below zero.

  • You set up a new item. Add it under Items / Categories. It starts at 0. If you already hold some, record an Opening balance adjustment (once per item).
  • Stock arrives. Use Add Stock: item, quantity, cost, supplier and bill number. The cost becomes the item's last purchase price.
  • You gave the wrong quantity or item on a stock-in. Void that stock-in (with a reason) and add the right one. A stock-in can not be voided if its stock has already been used.
  • Things are damaged, lost or expired. Record an Adjustment with the reason and a note. These take stock out.
  • A count shows a different number. Use Count correction and say whether the real count is higher or lower.
  • You hand items out. Use Issue / Return. Choose staff, a student or a department. If any line is short, nothing is issued.
  • Items come back. Open the issue and record the return. Good items go back into stock; damaged ones are recorded but are not added.
  • An issue was a mistake. Void it, but only while nothing has been returned.
  • Nothing is ever deleted. Items, categories and suppliers are deactivated instead, and keep their history.
  • Low stock means the quantity is at or below the item's minimum level (set the minimum to 0 for no alert).
  • The Principal can view the dashboard and items but can not change anything.
